## 4. THE THREE CORE TEACHINGS — FRAMING AND LANGUAGE

### Teaching 1: Hyper-Specific Messaging [18:00–25:00]
**Frame:** "This is the difference between a coaching business that does $1,000/mo and $10,000/mo"

Key concept — give prospects an "identity":
- ❌ "My client Sarah lost 15 pounds" → applies to any woman, resonates with none
- ✅ "This is Sondra, a career-driven mom of three" → specific identity creates resonance

Content reframe:
- ❌ "Here's how to lose weight"
- ✅ "Here's how to lose weight as a woman in your 30s"
- ❌ "3 tips for fat loss"
- ✅ "3 biggest mistakes that busy moms make on their fat loss journey"

**Soundbite:** *"The way that you've been posting and the way that you've been approaching your business is fundamentally wrong. If what you were doing was working, you would already be making more money."*

### Teaching 2: AI-Powered Content Creation [25:00–37:00]
**Frame:** "Great marketers join the conversation happening in prospects' minds. Previously you needed to understand marketing. Now you just need to understand AI."

The System:
1. Go to sandcastles.ai
2. Find competitor videos with 10X+ outlier score (last 3 months)
3. Copy transcript to Claude
4. Prompt: "Pull the template from this viral video script and give me the template so I can recreate it with my own spin"
5. Then: "Write me a viral video using this template towards [specific avatar]"

Live demo showed taking a "7 Instagram settings" video → rewriting it as "7 diet mistakes for ambitious women making $100K+"

**Soundbite:** *"I just took a video about Instagram settings, I pulled the template, and I rewrote the video for an ambitious working woman with a fucking prompt on my computer. And this is the stuff that's available to us RIGHT NOW."*

**Caveat seeded:** "It's also easy to screw up. If you don't have your avatar dialed in and you don't know how to prompt AI properly, you get AI slop." → Sets up the need for coaching.

### Teaching 3: DM Script for Booking Sales Calls [38:00–47:00]
**Frame:** "The easiest way to sign high-ticket fitness coaching clients"

The "5130 Post" story strategy:
> Post ONE story with a poll: "I'm looking for 5 men who want to drop 5-10 pounds in the next 30-60 days. I'm in / Send details / I'm interested / Nah, I'm good"
- "5" creates scarcity
- "Nah I'm good" option hacks algorithm (engagement from rejections)
- Delete all other stories — forces the algorithm to push this one

The DM Script (verbatim):
1. "Yo [name], saw you vote on the story. Got a couple questions, want to see if I can help."
2. "What would you say has been your number one struggle with fitness right now?"
3. [They reply] "Oh, losing 20 pounds, that makes sense. How long have you been struggling with that?"
4. [They reply] "Damn, two years, that's a long time. What would you say is your number one fitness goal?"
5. [They reply] → Send client before/after: "My client John started in a similar position. Would you like some help?"
6. [They ask what it looks like] "Tell you what — let's hop on a quick 20-30 min call. We'll go over your number one struggle and help you come up with a game plan. Are you up for that?"
7. [They say yes] "Cool, let me grab my calendar. [link] Go ahead and book in — spots fill up quick. Send me a screenshot and let me know what time you pick. Cool?"

Bad/Good/Excellent framework:
- **Bad:** Needy — word vomiting, chasing. "You chase, they run."
- **Good:** Transactional — follows the script but feels like a form
- **Excellent:** Conversational — "Talk like you're talking to your friend." Short answers, slang, casual tone
